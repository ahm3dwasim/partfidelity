A recorded comparison

The catch

When a redrawn image replaces a frame, the gate checks that the part is still the same part. It is strict. What follows is a recording of one measured run, not a live check: you cannot upload anything here.

What the record says

  • The real altered edit was rejected ($altered_rejected of $altered_total).
  • Held-out recipe corruptions: $recipes_rejected of $recipes_total rejected. The blur recipe is the known blind spot: $blur_accepted of $blur_total held-out blur cases got through, because the gate still finds the part.
  • The gate is strict. It also rejected $faithful_rejected of $faithful_total faithful edits, which is why redraws from Muse Image go to a person for a look rather than being trusted.
  • No paste-back composite result was recorded, so none is shown or claimed.
